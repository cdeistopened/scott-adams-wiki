# Prediction

> *Systems for anticipating outcomes*

## Overview

Rather than making point predictions, Scott advocates for systems thinking - putting yourself in a position where multiple outcomes lead to success. This domain covers his methods for evaluating claims, detecting hoaxes, and assessing risk.
